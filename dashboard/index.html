How the value is worked out

For each category, the model takes the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot's own live-token protocols in that category (three or more of them, else the median across all live-token protocols, ). A protocol's base value is its TVL times its category's ratio. Five adjustments follow, all from the snapshot's own figures:

  1. Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100%, feeds a factor of 1 + change × 0.6. A rising protocol is worth more; a falling one is trimmed.
  2. Tenure. The longer a protocol has run with no token, the more value has accrued before any launch: a factor of 1 + (days with no token, capped at 4 years) ÷ 4 years × 0.5. A protocol with no listing date is scored at 365 days.
  3. Breadth. Each extra chain a protocol runs on adds 0.04, up to five extra chains: more chains means more users and more integrations for a token to serve.
  4. Leadership. The protocol's share of its category's total TVL, capped at one half, feeds a factor of 1 + share × 0.25: the category leader's launch matters most.
  5. Momentum. The median 7-day change of live-token protocols in the same category, clamped to −20% … +50%, feeds a factor of 1 + change × 0.3. A category with too few live-token protocols takes the global median, . A rising category lifts its pre-token members.

Implied value = TVL × category ratio × growth × tenure × breadth × leadership × momentum, at least $1 and at most $50bn. It is this entry's claim, not a prediction: it does not say a token will be issued, or when. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry.

The board ranks every launch candidate the snapshot holds: all 40 protocols on its watchlist, plus every other candidate above and below the $50m floor. Left out are the records that are not launches at all: centralised exchanges, bridges, versioned pools, and wrapped or staked products of tokens that already trade, such as wrapped Bitcoin and bridged stablecoins. They hold large TVL, but they are not protocols waiting on a token of their own.
